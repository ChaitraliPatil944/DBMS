# 6. Common Mistakes

- Memorizing definitions without understanding.
- Ignoring SQL practice.
- Speaking too quickly.
- Guessing technical answers.
- Forgetting to ask clarifying questions.

# Final Takeaway

Placements are won through consistent preparation, not last-minute cramming. Review concepts, practice SQL, explain ideas aloud, and stay calm during interviews. A structured approach is more valuable than memorizing every possible question.
