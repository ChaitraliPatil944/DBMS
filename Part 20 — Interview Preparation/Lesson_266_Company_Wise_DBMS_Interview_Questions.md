# 1. Introduction

Different companies emphasize different DBMS topics.

- Service companies focus on fundamentals.
- Product companies test optimization and design.
- Startups often combine DBMS with coding and system design.

---

# Interview Insights ⭐

- Explain trade-offs instead of memorizing definitions.
- Draw ER diagrams for design questions.
- Mention indexing whenever discussing performance.
- Think aloud during scenario questions.

---

# Interview Traps 🚨

- Don't confuse sharding with replication.
- Don't claim indexes always improve performance.
- Don't normalize every database blindly; analytics systems often denormalize.

# Final Takeaway

Most companies evaluate the same DBMS fundamentals but emphasize different depths depending on the role. Master SQL, transactions, indexing, normalization, and database design before moving to advanced distributed database concepts.
